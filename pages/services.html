Our Services

Get accustomed to our services below.

Adoption Process

  1. Apply to meet a pet
  2. Meet the pet of your choice in person
  3. Complete a home safety and wellness check
  4. Take your pet home

Foster Program

Our foster program is a 12-week process where you home and take care of one of our homeless pets.

Volunteer Roles

  • Cage cleaner: cleans our animal cages
  • Dog walker: takes dogs on walks
  • Pet groomer: grooms and washes pets
  • Receptionist: answers the door and phone calls
