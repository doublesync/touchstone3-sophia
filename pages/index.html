Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is an animal shelter based in Twin Cities supporting animals all across the city! Feel free to donate to our cause by submitting a form.

Mission Statement

We want to introduce a time to the world where there are no homeless pets. We do this by putting an end to kill-shelters all around the globe, but starting here in Twin Cities.

Impact Statistics

  • 95% of our animals are adopted in less than a year
  • 25% of kill-shelters in Twin Cities have been shut down
  • There are 75% less homeless animals in Twin Cities this year compared to last year

How You Can Help

  1. Volunteer to help out around our shelter
  2. Sign-up to foster an animal without a home
  3. Treat animals with respect
