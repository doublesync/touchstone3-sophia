As you apply styles, follow these guidelines:

Use no more than 2 font families
Apply styles consistently across all pages
Ensure text is readable against background colors
Content should be easy to read and scan, meaning users can quickly skim headings, sections, and key information without reading every word.
Step 3: Implement Responsive Layout

Use a mobile-first approach when styling your pages
Use Flexbox to organize your layout across your pages
Add at least one media query to adjust layout for larger screens
Ensure content remains readable and usable across screen sizes
Focus on maintaining readability and clear structure as the layout changes.
